# Granada Briefing - Saturday 28 November 2026

**Today at a glance:** The Metro de Granada is on a full strike today, according to Granada Digital. Spain votes tomorrow, Sunday 29 November, in a general election. No verified AEMET forecast was available for Granada.

## Upcoming happenings

**Saturday 28 November**
- **Salón del Vehículo de Ocasión** - Fermasa, Granada. Runs 26-29 November. Over 1,000 used vehicles, with discounts of up to 5,000 euros. Time and entry price not stated. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/fermasa-feria-vehiculos-ocasion-2026/)
- **Sima Granada vs Cádiz** - Futsal league match marking the Granada side's return to league play, described as a demanding fixture. Venue and time not stated. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/sima-granada-cadiz-virgili-segunda-b-futbol-sala/)
- **Kerygma processions** - Live coverage of 45 brotherhood transfers (33 on shoulders, 12 on carts) across four main routes. Check the article for today's timings. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/traslados-kerygma-2026-directo/)

**Sunday 29 November**
- **General election** - Polls close at 8pm, with provisional results expected from about 9pm. The full official result waits for the overseas-vote count on Friday 4 December. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/join-our-live-qa-as-spain-heads-for-pivotal-general-election)
- **Salón del Vehículo de Ocasión** - Final day at Fermasa. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/fermasa-feria-vehiculos-ocasion-2026/)

## National news

- **Sánchez makes final push before vote** - Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez campaigned on the last day, 27 November, before Sunday's election. Polls put the PP ahead, likely leading to a hung parliament in which it could rely on Vox. The vote was called early after the right rejected urgent housing decrees in October. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/sanchez-makes-last-bid-to-beat-the-right-ahead-of-spanish-vote)
- **Election key questions** - The latest permitted poll (40dB, 23 November) had the PP at 31.8 percent, the PSOE at 30.9 percent and Vox at about 17.3 percent. A government needs 176 seats in Congress. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/join-our-live-qa-as-spain-heads-for-pivotal-general-election)
- **Márquez announces retirement** - Marc Márquez said at the Valencia circuit on 27 November that he is leaving MotoGP after 13 years. He is 33 and has had multiple right-arm and shoulder surgeries. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/spains-motogp-legend-marquez-announces-surprise-retirement)
- **Migrant deaths off the Canary Islands** - At least eight migrants have died in waters off the archipelago. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/at-least-8-migrants-die-off-spains-canary-islands)
- **Hernández de Cos to succeed Lagarde** - Spain's Hernández de Cos is set to take over as ECB chief. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/spains-hernandez-de-cos-to-succeed-lagarde-as-ecb-chief)
- **Capsized diesel ship off Ibiza** - The vessel is to be towed to port. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261127/capsized-ship-carrying-diesel-off-spains-ibiza-to-be-towed-to-port)
