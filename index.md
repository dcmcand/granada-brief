# Granada Briefing - Friday 27 November 2026

**Today at a glance:** Dry and clear, with a high of 14°C and a low of -2°C, according to [AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/eltiempo/prediccion/municipios/granada-id18087). Metro de Granada workers are on a full-day strike today and tomorrow, with 60% minimum services and trains about every 13 minutes instead of 6.5. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/huelga-total-metro-granada-27-28-noviembre/) Spain votes in a general election on Sunday 29 November.

## Local news

- **Metro strike hits Granada for two days** - Workers rejected a 41.2% pay offer for 2026-2030 from Avanza in a dispute over the collective agreement. Minimum services are set at 60%. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/huelga-total-metro-granada-27-28-noviembre/)
- **Thousands march against gender violence** - The 25 November march ran from Plaza del Triunfo to Plaza Nueva. Speakers criticised the regional government's policies and the lack of a women's information centre in the Montes Orientales for 694 days. No official attendance figure was given. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/granada-manifestacion-multitudinaria-25n/)
- **Kerygma exhibition ends with image transfers** - The 45 religious images return to their churches on Saturday morning. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/traslados-kerygma-granada-horarios-itinerarios/)
- **Subsidence on the A-44 ring road still unresolved** - The State road authority has repaired subsidence at the GR-30 near Maracena and La Chana, and at the GR-43 in Pinos Puente. The Alhendín-Las Gabias stretch has no lasting fix, and its speed limit was cut to 80 km/h over about 1.2 km in 2022. [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/hundimientos-a44-alhendin-las-gabias/)
- **Teatro Isabel la Católica unveils first-half 2027 season** - The 14 shows run from January to July. They open with "Fedra" starring Lydia Bosch (15-17 January), followed by "La Saga" with Fernando Tejero (19-21 February) and "Dulcinea" with Paloma San Basilio (5-6 March). [Granada Digital](https://www.granadadigital.es/teatro-isabel-catolica-programacion-2027/)
- **City Hall staff shortage dispute continues** - UGT held a stoppage and rally on 17 November at the Centro Cívico del Zaidín. The union has warned of a possible municipal strike before year-end if the next budget does not respond. [Motril Digital](https://www.motrildigital.news/granada-ugt-mantiene-las-protestas-por-la-falta-de-personal-municipal-y-advierte-de-una-posible-huelga-antes-de-fin-de-ano)
