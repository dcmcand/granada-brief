# Granada Briefing - Thursday 26 November 2026

**Today at a glance:** Granada is forecast to reach a high of 14 °C and a low of 1 °C, with dry, clear to partly cloudy skies and light north-westerly wind, according to [AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/en/eltiempo/prediccion/municipios/granada-id18087). AEMET lists no weather warnings for the Cuenca del Genil zone today, tomorrow or the day after. Spain holds a snap general election on Sunday 29 November.

## National news

- **Cold air mass brings snow warnings across northern Spain** - Snow is forecast above 900 metres in the north, and AEMET has issued orange alerts in Cantabria and the Basque Country. Frosts are expected to reach -2C in Ávila and Teruel on Friday. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261126/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-thursday-105)
- **Adamuz rail crash: forensic report points to broken weld** - Experts investigating the 19 January accident at Adamuz (Córdoba) name a break in a weld joining two track sections as one of the most likely causes. Their 81-page report has gone to the Montoro court. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261126/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-thursday-105)
- **Spain raises paid bereavement leave to ten days** - A royal decree law lifts leave for the death of a spouse, child, partner or first-degree relative from two to ten working days. Leave for other relatives rises to three working days, or five if travel to another province is needed. It also creates paid palliative care leave. It takes effect the day after publication in the BOE, likely today. Parliament must ratify it within 30 days, and the Standing Committee is expected to consider it on 3 December. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/spain-increases-bereavement-leave-for-workers-to-ten-days)
- **Snap general election on Sunday** - Only Spanish nationals aged 18 or over and on the electoral roll can vote. Foreign residents, including UK citizens, cannot vote in general elections. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261124/who-can-and-cant-vote-in-spains-general-election-on-sunday)
- **Exiled Catalan separatist leader plans return to Spain in December** - The Local reports the plan in a politics story published 25 November. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/exiled-catalan-separatist-leader-plans-return-to-spain-in-december)
- **Hard-left candidate calls for Beckham Law to be scrapped** - The Local reports the campaign call to end Spain's special tax regime for foreign workers. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/expats-pay-hard-left-leader-calls-for-spains-beckham-law-to-be-scrapped)

## Good to know

- **Voting on Sunday 29 November:** Foreign residents cannot vote in the general election. Polling day may affect opening hours and local services. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261124/who-can-and-cant-vote-in-spains-general-election-on-sunday)
- **Cold snap:** Sharp falls in temperature are forecast across Spain, with snow in the north and frosts inland. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261126/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-thursday-105)
