## Local news

- **Guardia Civil dismantles underground cannabis farm beneath a children's play park in Las Gabias** - Agents found a 180 m² facility with 1,954 plants. It was reached by a vehicle ramp hidden under artificial grass, a swing and a playhouse. Five people were arrested and three are under investigation. A judge ordered provisional prison for the alleged leader. The facility drew an illegal power connection equal to about 100 homes. [Infobae](https://infobae.com/espana/2026/11/25/el-bunker-debajo-de-un-parque-infantil-en-granada-que-ocultaba-una-plantacion-de-marihuana-subterranea-y-que-tenia-una-rampa-para-entrar-con-coche)
- **Operation "Nazarí-Sabika 04" seized 2,479 plants across the province** - Thirteen raids on 17 November covered Granada city, Albolote, Jun, Las Gabias and Maracena. Police also sealed a grow shop thought to have supplied several drug networks, and seized 22,970 € in cash. [Infobae](https://infobae.com/espana/2026/11/25/el-bunker-debajo-de-un-parque-infantil-en-granada-que-ocultaba-una-plantacion-de-marihuana-subterranea-y-que-tenia-una-rampa-para-entrar-con-coche)

## National news

- **Brussels warns Spain of possible excessive deficit procedure** - The European Commission estimates Spain will exceed its agreed 2026 spending limits by about €5 billion. EU officials blame energy price relief and public sector pay rises. The Commission will review official data next spring. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-wednesday-110)
- **Long queues mark Spaniards' early voting abroad** - Consulates report hours-long waits ahead of the 29 November general election. In-person voting abroad runs until 26 November. Ballots from 163,355 citizens naturalised under the "Grandchildren Law" go in separate boxes after the Supreme Court provisionally suspended their voting rights. Opposition leaders have questioned the transparency of those boxes. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-wednesday-110)
- **Record 2,121 migrants reach El Hierro in 24 hours** - Rescue services reported 15 boats, one death and a baby born at sea. Madrid approved an extra €1.5 million to clear abandoned boats from local ports. Canary Islands President Fernando Clavijo warned that arrivals will continue through December. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-wednesday-110)
- **"La Bola Negra" becomes Spain's highest-grossing film of 2026** - The film by Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi has taken €40.5 million, overtaking Spider-Man: Brand New Day. It is Spain's official Oscar submission for international feature. [The Local](https://www.thelocal.es/20261125/today-in-spain-a-roundup-of-the-latest-news-on-wednesday-110)
